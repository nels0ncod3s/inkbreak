# INKBREAK — Story Mode Chapter 3 LDD

## Chapter identity

**Chapter Three: THE DRAFTWORKS**

Target first-play duration: **10–14 minutes**. The chapter follows Chapter Two's discovery that The Artist was repairing erased geometry rather than creating corruption. The player enters the workshop that sits between source data and finished pages, restores three Line Anchors, survives a redaction event, and confronts the process that interrupted The Artist.

### Narrative question

If the player is a Borrowed Line whose source exists outside the current page, **who owns the source, and why is a Redaction process trying to sever it?**

### Chapter reveal

The Artist was not the author and was not the original threat. It is a repair entity reconstructing geometry from a deeper Source Layer. A process called **THE REDACTOR** was ordered to remove unsourced changes, including the player and potentially Mara.

---

## Level structure

The Draftworks is a wide workshop map rather than another corridor. It alternates long rifle sightlines with broken drafting bays, low tables, incomplete scaffolds and open central lanes.

### Location A — Arrival / Repair Bench

- Safe introduction area.
- Full-screen dialogue establishes that the workshop is unfinished by design.
- Player reaches a Repair Bench that exposes a partial Source Layer trace.
- The bench requests three Line Anchors before revealing the repair history.

**Objective:** `REACH THE REPAIR BENCH`

### Location B — Line Anchor One

- Mid-range combat space.
- Corrector + Flanker pair.
- Activating the anchor physically redraws a navigational strip into the environment.

**Objective:** `ACTIVATE LINE ANCHOR 1`

### Location C — Line Anchor Two

- Longer sightline zone.
- Sniper + Corrector + Rusher composition tests target prioritization.
- The second anchor restores more geometry and strengthens the Source trace.

**Objective:** `ACTIVATE LINE ANCHOR 2`

### Location D — Line Anchor Three

- Heavier encounter with Heavy + Corrector + Flanker.
- Completing the anchor sequence reveals that The Artist was reconstructing boundary damage.

**Objective:** `ACTIVATE LINE ANCHOR 3`

### Location E — Eraser Storm

- Movement-focused transition instead of a stationary firefight.
- Redaction slabs draw behind the player while Rushers/Flankers pressure the route.
- Contacts are disposable pressure; reaching the Foundry matters more than clearing every enemy.

**Objective:** `REACH THE DRAFT FOUNDRY`

### Location F — Draft Foundry

- Dialogue chamber / checkpoint.
- System text identifies the Redaction process as the entity tasked with removing unsourced changes.
- The player chooses confrontation rather than retreat.

### Location G — The Redactor

Boss-class guardian with high health, faster cadence and stronger damage than the Proofreader.

At ~55% HP, the arena performs a **Layout Purge** and draws new low cover, changing dash/slide lanes without spawning geometry directly on the player.

**Objective:** `BREAK THE REDACTOR`

### Location H — Source Gate

The Redactor's failure proves that the player's external source reference still exists. Mara concludes there may be another page or another version of the player. The Artist's last repair path leads beyond the Source Gate.

**Objective:** `ENTER THE SOURCE GATE`

Chapter ends on the line:

> “Once we cross, we stop following repairs. We start following whoever authored the damage.”

## Chapter-specific mechanics

### Line Anchors

Each anchor has three purposes:

1. Progress the Source Layer investigation.
2. Change the physical level by drawing new navigational geometry.
3. Trigger a deliberately composed enemy squad.

This makes the objective mechanically visible rather than another glowing terminal.

### Redaction geometry

Redaction events should never create blockers within the player's immediate safety radius. Dynamic geometry is authored to appear behind or lateral to the movement path, preserving the fantasy of a page rewriting itself without cheap unavoidable trapping.

### Environmental notes

Three optional notes are placed through the chapter:

- **FIRST HAND:** The Artist restores strokes from an inaccessible source layer.
- **BORROWED LINE:** Borrowed Lines survive correction because their source exists elsewhere.
- **MARA:** Mara predates the current page graph and may be a routing process or witness rather than a conventional resident.

---

## Boss: THE REDACTOR

### Role

The Redactor is not another Artist-sized bullet sponge. It represents the system enforcing page ownership.

### Phase 1 — Source Purge

- Guardian-style pressure.
- Medium-long engagement range.
- High cadence forces active cover changes.

### Phase 2 — Layout Purge

Triggered below ~55% health.

- New low cover is drawn into the Foundry.
- Existing firing lanes change.
- Player is encouraged to combine slide, dash, ADS and weapon switching.

### Death state

The standard INKBREAK cube breakup remains, but boss-class debris receives the larger pooled breakup treatment. The system reports:

`SOURCE PURGE FAILED. BORROWED LINE RETAINS EXTERNAL REFERENCE.`

## Chapter 4 handoff

Chapter Three should end with three unresolved threads:

- Who or what owns the player's external source?
- What interrupted The Artist's repair process?
- Is Mara a person, a copied voice, or a persistent annotation that learned to route itself between pages?

The next chapter should leave the conventional page graph and enter the **Source Layer**, allowing more abstract level geometry and stronger reality-breaking mechanics.
